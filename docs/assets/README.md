Create a wide landscape editorial brand illustration, 3:2 aspect ratio. Warm ivory paper background, restrained mint green, deep forest green and soft amber accents matching a cozy island research workspace. Center a memorable emblem of a small floating island shaped like an open book, one elegant sprouting leaf/tree, three tiny connected research lights suggesting branches and collaboration, precise clean vector-like forms with subtle tactile paper depth, refined and calm, ample negative space. Typography at bottom center exact English text 'RESEARCH ISLAND', smaller exact tagline 'Let ideas grow into answers.' No other text, no charts, no UI mockups, no third-party logos, no watermark. Strong design hierarchy, sophisticated open-source product identity, legible at README width. This is a product brand illustration, not a screenshot.
